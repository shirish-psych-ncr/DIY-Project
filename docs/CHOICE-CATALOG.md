# Therapist Dropdown and Phrase Catalog

Use these as therapist-facing choices for the existing Sheets modules. They are workflow scaffolding and neutral phrase starters, not diagnostic criteria, measure items, clinical findings, or treatment recommendations. Every list should include a blank/none/not-assessed route and allow clinician-authored text where the structured options do not fit.

## B. Intake domains and source labels

Use category plus source. For each item, record `Client report`, `Informant report`, `Existing record`, `Clinician observation`, `Not assessed`, or `Not applicable`.

- Presenting concern and client-stated goal
- Timeline, context, course, and changes
- Prior support and current care team
- Physical health, sleep, energy, appetite, and medications (as relevant to scope)
- Mood, worry, attention, coping, and daily functioning
- Relationships, family, peers, caregiving, and social supports
- Work, study, housing, finances, transport, and practical access
- Culture, language, identity, spirituality, and community context (client-defined)
- Strengths, interests, values, and sources of meaning
- Preferences, accommodations, privacy, and communication needs
- Client-selected safety/support discussion (clinician follow-up required where relevant)
- Other / not assessed / not applicable

## E. Case formulation / 5Ps scaffolding

Use separate narrative fields for client perspective, evidence/source, and therapist hypothesis.

- Presenting: current concern, client-described impact, and desired change
- Predisposing/contextual: developmental, health, learning, relational, or systemic context (only relevant, sourced information)
- Precipitating: events or changes temporally associated with current concern
- Perpetuating/maintaining: patterns, constraints, or unmet needs that may maintain difficulty (mark hypothesis)
- Protective/resources: strengths, relationships, skills, community, access, and prior successes
- Working hypothesis: one therapist-authored paragraph with uncertainty stated
- Alternative explanations / missing information
- Client's view of fit / correction to formulation
- Not assessed / not applicable / defer

## I. Risk and safety documentation status

This is an administrative workflow vocabulary, not a risk scale. Do not add formula-driven risk levels or infer safety status from general check-in answers.

- Not assessed this encounter
- Discussion indicated; clinician assessment documented in approved clinical record
- Further direct assessment planned (owner/date)
- Consult/escalation completed under clinic protocol (document action in approved record)
- Not applicable to this note
- Deferred / unable to assess (reason and plan)

Sensitive risk details and safety plans belong in the approved clinical record with appropriate access controls, not in a general dropdown catalog or notification.

## K. Phrase starters for generated notes

Use only when the corresponding source field exists; leave blank rather than filling missing information.

- `Client reported: [client's words or concise summary].`
- `Client identified [goal/priority] as the preferred focus for this session.`
- `Since the previous session, client described [change/context].`
- `Therapist observed [concrete behavior] during [context].`
- `The session included [intervention actually delivered]. Client response: [observed or reported response].`
- `Progress toward [client-selected goal]: [evidence and source].`
- `Barrier/access need identified: [barrier]. Agreed adjustment: [step/owner/date].`
- `Working formulation (therapist hypothesis): [synthesis]. Client's response/correction: [response].`
- `Next step agreed: [specific action, who, when, feasibility].`
- `Not assessed / deferred: [domain] because [reason]. Planned follow-up: [owner/date].`
- `Coordination: [purpose, recipient/role, consent, date, outcome].`

## L. Source-library mapping

The local scales library suggests therapist choice domains including mood, anxiety/worry, panic, sleep, somatic concerns, coping, emotion regulation, functioning, quality of life, values, trauma, substance use/recovery, neurodevelopment, cognition, eating, relationships, and developmental context. These labels can inform searchable navigation and measure selection. Use the exact measure-specific source and rights/scoring record for any instrument. Do not convert domain labels into diagnoses or copy instrument items into this catalog.
